# References

Authority order: the primary papers below > `pct-theorem.md` > any other note. When a Lean statement
is claimed to formalise a result, cite the paper and the theorem/equation number in the Issue and PR.

**Cite a preprint by version number.** A statement checked against an arXiv preprint must name the
version, because the versions differ in ways that matter: [T26] arXiv v1 displays the `C^N` norm as
`Σ_{j=1}^{N}`, where v2 and the published article display `Σ_{j=0}^{N}`, and v1 carries several
typos that v2 repairs. The published article is authoritative; the matching preprint version is
recorded with each entry below.

- **[T26]** J. E. Tener, *The Bisognano–Wichmann property for non-unitary Wightman conformal field
  theories*, Comm. Math. Phys. 407 (2026), no. 6, paper no. 128; preprint
  [arXiv:2506.10625v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10625v2), which agrees with the published text on
  every statement this repository relies on. — Primary source for the non-unitary Wightman framework
  and the PCT theorem in its most general form.
- **[CRTT25]** S. Carpi, C. Raymond, Y. Tanimoto, J. E. Tener, *Non-unitary Wightman CFTs and
  non-unitary vertex algebras*, Sel. Math. New Ser. 31 (2025), no. 4, paper no. 66; preprint
  [arXiv:2409.08454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08454). — Equivalence
  between Möbius vertex algebras and Möbius-covariant Wightman CFTs; well-definedness of the
  correspondences. Cited by [T26] v1 as `[CRTT24]` and by v2 as `[CRTT25]`; both are this paper.
- **[RTT22]** C. Raymond, Y. Tanimoto, J. E. Tener, *Unitary vertex algebras and Wightman conformal
  field theories*, Comm. Math. Phys. 395 (2022), no. 1, 299–330. — Unitary case; the PCT operator `θ`.
- **[CKLW18]** S. Carpi, Y. Kawahigashi, R. Longo, M. Weiner, *From vertex operator algebras to
  conformal nets and back*, Mem. Amer. Math. Soc. 254 (2018), no. 1213. — Conformal nets versus
  unitary vertex operator algebras; origin of the smeared-field constructions.
